WcbSerialHub — 2-tab port sharing test

Open this page in two tabs of the same origin. Click Join in both. Then click Pick Port in one tab and choose your MgmtRelay/WCB — that tab becomes the LEADER (a tab holding no port never campaigns for the lock, so picking is what promotes it). The other stays FOLLOWER. Type in either tab → it reaches the one port; the board's replies appear in both. Close the leader tab → the follower auto-promotes and re-opens the port.
