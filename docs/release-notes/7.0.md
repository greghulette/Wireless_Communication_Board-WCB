# WCB 7.0

The first release since 6.2.1 (September 2026). WCBs can now join or host WiFi, stored sequences get a store of their own, and the firmware has been through a bench test harness of 1,400+ tests on real boards, which found and fixed over a hundred defects along the way.

## Upgrading from 6.2.1

**Your settings and stored sequences come through.** A board on 6.2.1 updated to 7.0 keeps every setting and every sequence. On the bench, a board configured on 6.2.1 kept all 70 of its settings and all 26 of its sequences, which then moved on their own into the new sequence store.

- **Update the firmware the usual way.** Use the Wizard's **Update Firmware**, Intellex's flash, or OTA through a relay. These update the app only, and the board keeps its settings. A **Factory Reset** (or Intellex's **Full Wipe & Flash**) erases them, as before.
- **Use the 7.0 Wizard with 7.0 boards.** The Wizard on the web updates with this release. If you use **Intellex**, press **Update tools** (or update Intellex) so it carries the new Wizard. An older Wizard still works with 7.0 boards except for one thing: a board's remote terminal goes quiet after 30 seconds (see below).
- **Going back to 6.2.1:** the first 7.0 boot moves every sequence out of the settings storage into the new sequence store, which 6.2.1 cannot read. A board taken back to 6.2.1 therefore has **no stored sequences**, not even the ones it had on 6.2.1. Take a `?backup` on 7.0 first and paste its `?SEQ,SAVE` lines back afterwards (6.2.1 may refuse a very long one). Your other settings are unaffected.
- **Partition layout is unchanged** (`min_spiffs`), so no full reflash is needed.

## What's new

### WiFi
- **A WCB can host an access point or join an existing network**, on the mesh's own channel, so ESP-NOW keeps working: `?WIFI,AP,<ssid>,<pass>`, `?WIFI,JOIN,<ssid>,<pass>`, `?WIFI,OFF`, and `?WIFI` for the status. WiFi is **off** unless you turn it on.
- **A command console over WiFi** at `ws://<board>/ws`, for two clients at once. A slow or vanished client can no longer stall the board or exhaust its memory.
- **WiFi settings on the Wizard's board card.**

### Stored sequences
- **A sequence store of its own**, separate from the settings storage: up to **32 KB of sequences**, each up to **3,999 characters**. 6.2.1 kept sequences in the shared settings storage, where a long one could fail to save with "NVS write rejected". 7.0 doesn't have that problem.
- **The Wizard shows sequences a page at a time** (5/10/25/50/All), with a filter on name or contents, and how much of the 32 KB store is used.
- **A sub-sequence called twice in one sequence runs both times.** 6.2.1 refused the second call as a loop. A sequence that really does call itself, directly or through others, is still refused.
- **`?NVS`** (new) shows how full the settings storage and the sequence store are.

### Mesh and management
- **Large configs pull in parts.** A board whose config is over 2,912 characters can now be pulled through a relay.
- **Remote terminals end on their own** 30 seconds after the last renewal. The Wizard renews them every 12 seconds while a board is managed, so a page that was closed no longer leaves boards mirroring their consoles over the mesh forever.
- **Serial-attached devices** (WDP-DA) are saved, shared with every board, and can be forgotten.
- **ESP-NOW sends are paced**, and a board says when its transmit stalls and when it recovers.
- **Many ETM fixes:** boot announces, duplicate filtering, ACK accounting, and refusing what the command queue can't take instead of acknowledging and dropping it.

### Reliability (from the bench harness)
- **PWM output pulses are clocked by hardware**, so other work can no longer stretch them.
- **Soft serial S3–S5:** a level-triggered receiver, a first line after boot that arrives whole, and baud changes that can't crash a board short of memory. An HCR or a WLED can now sit on S3–S5 at up to 57,600 baud (9,600 before). `?BAUD` refuses rates above 115,200 there, which those ports can't carry: use S1 or S2 for those.
- **HCR:** a status readback that works on S3–S5, and a graceful stop that really is graceful.
- **Kyber and Maestro:** ports a device has taken are respected by mappings and by each other, and the Kyber setup line uses each Maestro's own baud.
- **A line too long for the free memory is dropped whole**, with a message, instead of being cut. Lines are capped at 4,096 characters on S1–S5 and 32,768 on USB (where a whole `?backup` gets pasted).
- **Maestro subroutine numbers are 0–127**, the Maestro's own range. A larger number is ignored instead of going out as a corrupt frame.
- **Timer chains and the LED pin are stricter.** `?LED,PIN` refuses pins that would lock a board out.

### Diagnostics
- **A board whose main loop stops says where:** a `[HANG] loop() stuck …` line on its console after 15 seconds.
- **`?DEBUG,MGMT`** now carries the management chatter (statistics reports, remote-terminal renewals), so **`?DEBUG,ON`** shows only the commands you're debugging.
- **Test-only fault switches** (`?DEBUG,FAULT,…`) let the bench drive failure paths a working board never reaches. Don't use them on a droid: most are cleared by a reboot, but three deliberately damage saved settings or a stored sequence so the test can check the repair.

## Known issues
- **A computer joining a WCB's access point can wait up to 47 seconds for an address** ([fix tracker](https://github.com/greghulette/Wireless_Communication_Board-WCB/blob/main/docs/HIL_FIX_TRACKER.md) item 110). The cause is still being looked for.
- **A board's ESP-NOW transmit can stall after heavy load, until it is rebooted** ([fix tracker](https://github.com/greghulette/Wireless_Communication_Board-WCB/blob/main/docs/HIL_FIX_TRACKER.md) item 112). It's rare. NaviCore now detects it and recovers on its own, but a WCB doesn't yet, and it says so on its console when it happens.
- **A WCB going silent mid-run** was seen twice on the bench in long test runs and couldn't be reproduced on demand. The new loop watch will show where it stopped if it happens again.

## Compatibility
- **NaviCore:** works with the current NaviCore firmware, built with WCB_Client 1.18.1 and WcbCmd 0.9.2.
- **The Wizard and Intellex:** use the 7.0 Wizard. Intellex users: **Update tools**, or Intellex 0.2.1 or later.
