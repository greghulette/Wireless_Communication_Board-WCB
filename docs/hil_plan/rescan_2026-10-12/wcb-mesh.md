## Notes

- Status 'tested' means a listed test's assertion fails when the behaviour breaks; each listed test body was read.
- Tests from other slices are cited where they assert a mesh rule (input.*, map.*, pwm.p_forwarded_stats, serial.timer_stop_mesh, if.payload_rejections); their non-mesh halves are audited in their own slices.
- The WDP device-announce (WDP-DA) auto-configuration, wdpCapOwner routing and the Maestro-remote auto-enable are left to the devices slice, as the brief says.
- Opt-in rows (mesh_password, nvs_erase, etm_seq_wrap) count as tested when run; they are not part of an unattended full run.
