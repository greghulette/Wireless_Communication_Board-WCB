## Notes

- A behaviour counts as tested only where a test's assertion fails when it breaks (test bodies read, not titles).
- Opt-in and attended entries are tested when their test asserts the behaviour; they do not run in an unattended pass.
- Mesh-side relays of sequences (?MGMT,SEQ/SEQGET) are listed once (seq.inventory_relay); the mesh slice owns them.
